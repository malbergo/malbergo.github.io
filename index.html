About

I am a Junior Fellow at the Society of Fellows at Harvard and an IAIFI Fellow at MIT.

Beginning in July 2026, I will be an assistant professor of Applied Mathematics at Harvard and a Kempner Institute Investigator. If you are a prospective graduate student or postdoc interested in working with me, please reach out detailing your research interests.

I am seeking highly motivated graduate students and postdocs to join my group starting in Fall 2026. Please apply at this link and write to me that you are applying. We will work on principled algorithms that make generative models scalable, understandable, and useful across the sciences and beyond.

If you are interested in a postdoc, please reach out to me directly and consider applying to the Kempner Institute Research Fellowship.
