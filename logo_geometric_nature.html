01 / Phyllotaxis

Golden angle phyllotaxis — the mathematical pattern of seed arrangement in sunflowers, pinecones, and spiraling galaxies

02 / Voronoi

Voronoi tessellation — the organic partition of space found in cell structures, leaf veins, and giraffe spots

03 / Branching

Recursive branching — the self-similar fractal structure of trees, river deltas, neural networks, and lungs

04 / Fibonacci

Fibonacci spiral — the golden ratio appearing in nautilus shells, hurricanes, galaxies, and facial proportions

05 / Hexagonal

Hexagonal packing — the most efficient 2D tessellation found in honeycombs, basalt columns, and insect eyes

06 / Radial

Radial symmetry — the 5-fold pattern of starfish, flowers, and many marine organisms (echinoderms)
