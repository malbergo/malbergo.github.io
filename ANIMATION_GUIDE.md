# Where to See the Animations

## 🎬 Always Visible (No Interaction Required)

### Homepage Background - **Floating Particles** ⭐ NEW!
**What to watch for**: Minimalist red-outlined geometric shapes slowly drifting across the background
- **When**: Immediately upon loading the homepage
- **Duration**: Continuous (particles loop every 45-70 seconds)
- **How subtle**: VERY subtle (8% opacity) - like a gentle texture
- **What they look like**: Squares, circles, rectangles, and lines in red outline
- **Speed**: Slow, meditative drift across the viewport

**To see them clearly**: Look at the background behind the text. You'll notice faint red geometric shapes slowly moving from left to right or diagonally.

---

### Bio Section - **Fractal Zoom (Breathing)**
**What to watch for**: The bio section (with red left border) gently grows and shrinks
- **When**: Continuously on the homepage
- **Duration**: 8-second loop
- **How subtle**: VERY subtle (2% scale change: 1.0 ↔ 1.02)
- **Effect**: Like the section is breathing

---

### Section Headers - **Binary Pulse**
**What to watch for**: "News" and "Research Papers" headers gently fade in/out
- **When**: Continuously on all pages
- **Duration**: 3-second loop
- **How subtle**: Moderate (opacity: 1.0 ↔ 0.7)
- **Effect**: Like a blinking cursor or heartbeat

---

### Page Load - **Spiral Fade-in**
**What to watch for**: Papers, news items, and photos rotate + scale as they appear
- **When**: When you first load or refresh any page
- **Duration**: 0.6-1.2 seconds per element
- **Effect**: Content spirals into view with the Fibonacci ratio
